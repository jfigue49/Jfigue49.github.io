Getting Started with Homeschooling

Starting your homeschool journey can feel overwhelming at first, but you do not have to figure everything out at once. Taking it one step at a time can help you create a homeschool experience that works for your family.

Start with Your Goals

Think about what you want your homeschool experience to look like. Consider your child's learning needs, your family's schedule, and the goals you have for the school year. Every family is different, so your homeschool does not have to look exactly like someone else's.

Choose Your Curriculum

There are many curriculum options available for homeschool families. Before purchasing anything, think about your child's grade level, learning style, your teaching preferences, and your budget. You can also combine different resources instead of using one curriculum for every subject.

Create a Simple Routine

A homeschool routine can provide structure without making every day feel too strict. Start with your core subjects and build your schedule around your family's needs. Remember to leave room for breaks, reading, hands-on activities, and time to explore your child's interests.

Give Yourself Time to Adjust

Your homeschool routine may change as you learn what works best for your family. It is okay to adjust your schedule, try different resources, or change your approach along the way. Homeschooling gives families the flexibility to create a learning environment that grows with their children.
